## Limitations (stated explicitly, not resolved by this audit)
  - Vendor data revisions: Yahoo Finance can silently revise historical OHLCV (e.g. corrected closes) between the original experiment-5 fetch and this fixture's capture -- any such revision would surface as a value mismatch above, indistinguishable from a genuine reproduction failure without a third independent source.
